Agency rubric — supplement
Fold into your existing interview scoring rubric. Each panelist scores 1 (weak) / 2 (acceptable) / 3 (strong) per dimension, plus written evidence. "Strong" looks different at different levels.
Dimension
Strong (IC / junior)
Strong (senior / manager+)
Initiative
Has shipped something nobody asked for. Identifies gaps in their own work.
Has expanded scope without permission. Reshapes mandates rather than executing them.
Verification
Tests their own output before showing it. Catches their own mistakes.
Builds verification into systems. Their team's output is independently reliable.
Taste
Can articulate why one approach is better than another.
Sets the standard for the team. Their bar is widely cited inside the org.
Question quality
Asks questions that reframe the conversation.
Their questions to the team improve over time. Has stopped asking some questions.
Feedback metabolism
Names a recent mistake concretely. Updates behavior, not just words.
Has changed a public position on a substantive matter based on evidence.
Ambiguity tolerance
Has shipped under uncertainty. Doesn't wait for the spec to be complete.
Operates teams through extended ambiguity without freezing or thrashing.
Ownership
Says "I" for losses, not just wins. Names what they could have done differently.
Owns outcomes across functions, not just their own scope.
Scope expansion
Has volunteered for hard problems outside their lane.
Has negotiated a meaningful expansion of mandate before. Multiple times.
How to use
Add these 8 dimensions to your existing rubric. Don't replace what's there — the technical / functional dimensions you already score still matter. Agency is the addition that distinguishes between two functionally-equivalent candidates and predicts how they'll grow.
Verdict heuristic: a candidate scoring 1 on Ownership or Feedback metabolism is a no-hire regardless of other scores. A candidate scoring 3s on Initiative, Taste, and Question quality is hireable even with a slightly weaker functional skillset — they'll close the gap.
